# Possible improvements (NOT implemented) - in priority order

## High
1. **Softer hit slowdown**: going from ~100 to ~50 px/s on a hit (especially in the cart) feels like a hard brake. Ease it in over 0.3 s and slow down less in the cart.
2. **Overlapping popups**: queue the important popups (PIU VELOCE, SCAMBI, CAMPANACCIO, record) so they don't stack. Max 1 large popup at a time.
3. **Junction readability in the cart**: show the green signal / red X earlier (now ~2 s), plus a short vibration/sound when you're on a broken track. Better on small phones.
4. **Collectibles on the HUD**: a small cowbell/facciola counter that appears when you pick one up (now only the popup), plus a "+1" flying towards the counter.
5. **Run start**: the speed goes from 34 to 82 almost instantly. A gentler 1 s ramp-up after the GIOCA countdown.

## Medium
6. **Real seated pose in the cart** (a dedicated clip: legs bent, hands on the edge) instead of the standing pose lowered into the cart.
7. **Cart: clearer camera**: slightly higher and further back during the ride to see 2 junctions ahead.
8. **Menu at 360 px**: 5 tiles are tight. Option: 4 tiles plus COSTUMI as a badge next to the record (with a dot when a skin can be unlocked).
9. **COSTUMI**: 3D skin preview (rotating runner) instead of the icon; "NEW" notice when you have enough to unlock.
10. **Second skin**: Sa Grastula (approved, next) with its own ability (e.g. a magnet for nearby materials, since there is no jump).
11. **Audio**: cart wheel/rail sound that changes with speed, metal clank at junctions, echo in the tunnel.
12. **Biome transitions**: brief name card of the place ("Funtana Raminosa") on entry, like the dance's.

## Low
13. **Mobile performance**: the village has ~390k triangles and ~290 draw calls. Merge the facades and lower the LOD of distant houses.
14. **Bonfires (falo)**: side villagers are small. Make them 15-20% bigger and add a small light on the cobblestones.
15. **Game over**: show the run's collectibles (cowbells/facciole) next to the carpets.
16. **Accessibility**: option to reduce screen shake and flashes; bigger text option.
17. **Balance**: the Stramaioni's +8% speed also increases the danger. Add a small points bonus (+5%) to make it worth it.
